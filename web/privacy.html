Privacy policy

The controller is SimteGen, s.r.o., company ID (IČO) 13980351, VAT ID CZ13980351, registered office Korunní 2569/108, Vinohrady, 101 00 Praha 10, Czech Republic; registered with the Municipal Court in Prague, file no. C 357544. Contact: support@simtegen.com.

This website stores no cookies or anything else in your browser and uses no analytics or advertising tools.

The website is hosted on Cloudflare Pages; loading a page creates the hosting provider's usual technical records (IP address, time of the request), kept for a short period, at most 30 days. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in running the website and keeping it secure (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

If you write to us through the Report a problem button, we send your message with technical details about the page (screen, window size, browser) encrypted to our support records, kept with GitHub, Inc. Only our company reads it, and only to deal with it. Write only what is needed — personal data is not required. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in fixing what does not work (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR); we keep the message only as long as dealing with it takes.

If you send a note through the form Missing an app? What bugs you?, we send its text (and the trade or hobby, if you fill it in) the same way, encrypted, to our company. We do not ask for your name or e-mail. We use the text only to find problems worth building an app for: we summarise it in our own words and keep the original text for at most 12 months. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in learning which problems people have (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

If you e-mail us (for example at support@simtegen.com), our company receives the message and keeps a copy of it — sender, subject and text, without attachments — encrypted in its support records with GitHub, Inc. The message is sorted, and ordinary questions are answered, by our company's automated assistant using artificial intelligence (Anthropic); legal matters and anything the assistant cannot answer with certainty are handled by a person. Replies are sent through the Resend service. We keep the message for at most 12 months; you can ask us at the same address to delete it sooner. Legal basis: dealing with your request — steps before or under a contract with you (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) — and otherwise our legitimate interest in answering the mail we receive (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Transfers outside the EU

Our processors Cloudflare, Inc., GitHub, Inc., Resend (Plus Five Five, Inc.) and Anthropic, PBC are companies based in the United States, so your data may be accessed from there. Such transfers are covered by the European Commission's standard contractual clauses (Art. 46(2)(c) GDPR), part of their data processing agreements, or by the EU–US Data Privacy Framework where the processor is certified under it. We will send you a copy of the safeguards on request.

Your rights

You have the right to access your data, to have it rectified or erased, to restrict its processing and to object to processing based on our legitimate interest (Art. 21 GDPR) — write to support@simtegen.com; we answer within one month. You may also lodge a complaint with the Czech data protection authority, Úřad pro ochranu osobních údajů, Pplk. Sochora 27, 170 00 Praha 7 (uoou.gov.cz).

Each of our apps has its own privacy policy, in Czech and English; you will find it inside the app.
